Abstract

AgentOSCIN is an OWL ontology for describing agentic AI systems, extending the Agentic AI Ontology (agentO) with vocabulary for orchestration, multi-agent coordination, and reasoning patterns. It models how LLM agents and teams are organized into workflows, bound to memory, guarded by validation and human-checkpoint controls, and terminated under configurable conditions. Built on AgentO (https://w3id.org/agentic-ai/onto), AgentOSCIN provides a common vocabulary for representing agent goals, tools, capabilities, and workflow steps across different agentic frameworks.
