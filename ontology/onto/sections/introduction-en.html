Introduction back to ToC

AgentOSCIN is developed as a targeted extension of AgentO. AgentOSCIN add layers containing the vocabulary needed to describe agentic-system Orchestration, Coordination, and reasoning patterns, memory bindings, guardrails, human checkpoints, and termination conditions — together with datatype properties (e.g. hasSourceFramework, hasFrameworkAttribute) that record which framework-specific construct (a CrewAI @listen decorator, an AutoGen role, a LangGraph node) each ontology individual was derived from, so that a system's original implementation can be faithfully reconstructed from its ontological representation. The goal of this work is thus twofold: (i) to provide a framework-agnostic, semantically grounded vocabulary that captures the orchestration and control-level aspects of agentic AI systems not covered by existing ontologies, and (ii) to preserve the framework provenance within that vocabulary to support round-trip translation between concrete agentic-system implementations and their ontological representation to allow for documentation, comparison, and transformation of agentic AI systems independent of the framework in which they were built.
